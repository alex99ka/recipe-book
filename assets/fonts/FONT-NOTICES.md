The app bundles these unmodified font families from Google Fonts:

Hebrew: Rubik, Noto Sans, Noto Serif, Heebo, Assistant, Alef, Frank Ruhl Libre,
David Libre, Secular One, and Open Sans.

English and Russian: Roboto, Open Sans, Rubik, Montserrat, Noto Sans, Inter,
Source Sans 3, Merriweather, Playfair Display, and Noto Serif.

Each new font's SIL Open Font License text is bundled in `licenses/` next to
the font files. Google Fonts source files and copyright details are published
at https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl . The existing Rubik and Noto
license texts are included alongside their font files.
